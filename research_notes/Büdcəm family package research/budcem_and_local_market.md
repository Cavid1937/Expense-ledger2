## 2. Features, plans, prices and payment

### Takeaway
Two paid plans billed through Apple / Google in USD: Personal ("Premium") $1.99/month or $17.99/year, Family $2.99/month or $24.99/year. Website says 7-day free trial; a recent App Store release note mentions a 24-hour trial for new users — conflicting. What, if anything, works without paying is not stated.

### Cited Findings
- App Store in-app purchases: Büdcəm Premium Monthly $1.99, Premium Yearly $17.99, Family Monthly $2.99, Family Yearly $24.99 — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/az/app/b%C3%BCdc%C9%99m/id6807151015)
- Website: "Şəxsi" $1.99/month and "Ailəm" $2.99/month, both "first 7 days free", "cancel anytime", yearly "more economical" (no yearly price shown); payment via Apple account; "no card needed to sign up" — [budcem.az](https://budcem.az)
- App Store release notes mention "a 24-hour free trial for new users" — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/az/app/b%C3%BCdc%C9%99m/id6807151015). Conflicts with the 7-day claim on the website and in the 17 Nov video ad.
- Terms: subscriptions bought via App Store / Google Play only; auto-renew; refunds only under store rules; deleting the account does not cancel the subscription or refund it — [terms](https://budcem.az/terms.html)
- Feature list (website): real-time income/expense tracking with auto-categorisation; AI Advisor (spending by category, month-over-month change, next-month forecast, Q&A over own data, "24/7"); daily limit alerts by push; family budget; savings goals with daily target; loans (remaining debt, monthly payment, next date); subscriptions; mood tag ("Əhval"); quick entry with category, payment method, mood; multiple currencies (€, ₺, $); onboarding questionnaire producing a budget plan; "100% data privacy" — [budcem.az](https://budcem.az)
- Feature list (App Store description): card payments logged automatically from bank notifications; cash added manually, by voice, or by receipt scan; budget limits, category limits; smart alerts (daily limit, budget overrun, unusual purchase); family budget; goals (emergency fund, travel, car, home, education); accounts/balances; loans and debts; subscriptions; history — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/az/app/b%C3%BCdc%C9%99m/id6807151015)

## 3. Family package ("Ailəm" / "Ailə paketi") specifics

### Takeaway
Family = owner + up to 4 members (5 total), joined by an invitation link; owner can remove members and sets permissions; personal transactions are not shown to the family automatically; each member clearly has their own Büdcəm account (phone-number login). Exact permission toggle names were not found in any public text.

### Cited Findings
- Up to 5 people including the owner (1 owner + 4 members); owner creates the family space, invites and removes members — [terms](https://budcem.az/terms.html); website says "up to 5 members", "member permissions", "family panel and member balances", "push notifications for family members", "includes everything in the personal plan" — [budcem.az](https://budcem.az)
- Joining: "Family invitation links should be shared only with people you know" ⇒ invite-link mechanism — [terms](https://budcem.az/terms.html)
- Permissions: members can contribute to the family budget; management permissions follow the owner's permission settings — [terms](https://budcem.az/terms.html)
- Privacy between members: family-space transactions visible to other members "within their access level"; personal transactions not shown automatically; owner may have broader access; whether a member's full salary vs. the amount allocated to family is visible is controlled by in-app permissions — [privacy](https://budcem.az/privacy.html)
- Family data (membership, roles, permissions, family transactions) is processed only when Family Mode is on — [privacy](https://budcem.az/privacy.html)
- Leaving: in-app; member keeps personal budget; access to shared data ends. If owner's subscription ends, members may lose the family space but keep personal budgets — [terms](https://budcem.az/terms.html); [privacy](https://budcem.az/privacy.html)
- Edge rules: buying Personal does not remove an existing family membership; buying Family does not make you owner of an existing group — [terms](https://budcem.az/terms.html)
- Accounts: login is phone number + SMS one-time code, or Apple/Google sign-in; no passwords — [privacy](https://budcem.az/privacy.html) ⇒ every member needs their own account/phone.
- Ad (17 Nov 2026, supplied by requester): two budgets in one app with a top switch; "Kim nəyi görür — sən seçirsən"; add/remove members; shared goals ("Ortaq hədəflər"), limits, loans + cash + cards on one panel.

### Inferences
- Sync is server-based (data on their servers, offline queue banner) — so family sharing is real-time-ish when online; offline edits wait. Conflict handling between members is not documented.
- Family loans/cards/cash are most likely entered manually or via each member's own bank-notification capture; there is no bank API integration.
- "Hide family panel from children" in the ad implies per-member visibility toggles; children would need a phone and an account (age floor 13 per older note's reading of the privacy text — not re-verified here).

### Gaps
- Exact toggle names, roles beyond owner/member, invitation expiry, whether members can be read-only: not found in public text. Store screenshots were not readable via fetch; owner screenshots of the Family screen would close this.
- Server location: not stated; "data may be processed outside Azerbaijan" — [privacy](https://budcem.az/privacy.html)

## 4. Bank SMS / notifications, bank connections, AI

### Takeaway
Büdcəm reads bank push notifications (optional), sends their content to its server to extract amount/currency/merchant/last card digits/balance; no direct bank connection to Kapital/Birbank, ABB, Leobank, Unibank or PASHA was found. AI uses Google Gemini (analysis) and Anthropic (receipt/debt OCR, text entry, a Telegram AI feature).

### Cited Findings
- Optional notification processing on the server; extracted fields: amount, currency, merchant description, last card digits, balance; original text not kept in diagnostic logs — [privacy](https://budcem.az/privacy.html)
- App Store description: "card payments logged automatically from bank notifications" — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/az/app/b%C3%BCdc%C9%99m/id6807151015)
- Reviewer praise for automatic Apple Pay / Google Pay payment capture (RamazanovN, 26 Oct) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/az/app/b%C3%BCdc%C9%99m/id6807151015)
- Google Gemini for analysis/recommendations; Anthropic for OCR of receipts/debt images, text-based entry and Telegram AI; Anthropic retention ~30 days; data may leave Azerbaijan — [privacy](https://budcem.az/privacy.html)
- AI disclaimer: results "may be inaccurate, incomplete or unsuitable"; not licensed financial advice — [terms](https://budcem.az/terms.html)
- SMS reading: not mentioned anywhere; SMS appears only as one-time login codes — [privacy](https://budcem.az/privacy.html)

### Inferences
- Which banks' notification formats are supported is not published; on iOS, notification reading is not technically possible for third-party apps, so the iOS "automatic" capture likely relies on Apple Shortcuts/Wallet automations (as Cashera does). Hypothesis, not verified.

### Gaps
- List of supported banks; how iOS capture actually works.

## 7. Local landscape: family features at banks/fintechs and other budgeting apps

### Takeaway
Azerbaijani banks offer child cards with parental control (Leobank Uşaq kartı, ABB Alpha Card), not household budgeting. The only other local budgeting app with a family plan found is Cashera (Plus Family, 2–5 people, 7.99 ₼/month or 59 ₼/year), which also has free split groups. No Birbank/Kapital Bank child or family product was found.

### Cited Findings
- Leobank (Unibank's digital bank) Uşaq kartı: ages 7–18, one parent applies, free card and delivery, IDs of parent and child; ordered in app via "Menyu → Uşaq kartı aç" — [Leobank FAQ](https://leobank.az/az/faq/kids-card); [marja.az](https://w.marja.az/130253/leobank-usaq-kartini-teqdim-edir-erken-yasdan-maliyye-savadliligi)
- Leobank child-card controls: spending limits, real-time notifications, category blocking, cash-withdrawal ban (press description) — [ena.az](https://ena.az/az/news/id-67068)
- Leobank child-card users reached 20,000 (announced Aug 2026); Leobank >1.5 million customers — [fed.az](https://fed.az/az/bank/leobank-usaq-karti-istifadecilerinin-sayi-20-000-e-catdi-263025); [trend.az](https://az.trend.az/business/4214692.html)
- ABB Alpha Card for children 7–17, ordered in ABB mobile; card opened in parent's name for child's use; parent controls/limits and real-time notifications (article ≈ spring 2025, may be outdated) — [apa.az](https://apa.az/maliyye/abb-alfa-nesli-ucun-alpha-card-mehsulunu-teqdim-edib-896355)
- ABB "Uşaq yığım" child savings deposit exists — [apa.az](https://apa.az/finance/abb-den-usaq-yigim-emaneti-1006251)
- Legal frame: a minor's card is an additional card tied to the parent's card; under-18 accounts only with parent/guardian; debit only — [azedu.az](https://azedu.az/az/news/96085/usaqlar-ve-bank-kartlari-her-yas-ucun-huquqi-cercive-necedir-qanun)
- Birbank: card pages show extra cards (Birbank Cashback: first two per currency free, then 15 ₼ each) but no child/family product found — [cards.birbank.az](https://cards.birbank.az/en/BCPC/bb)
- Cashera: Free 0; Plus 4.99 ₼/39 ₼; Plus Family 7.99 ₼/59 ₼ for 2–5 people, each member gets Plus, shared group and expenses visible to all immediately, one payment, change members anytime; free split groups up to 5 with equal/custom split and "who owes whom"; Android notification reading, iOS via Shortcut, no bank connection; statement import PDF/CSV/Excel; operator ProTech Solutions Group MMC; pricing in AZN by bank card — [cashera.app](https://cashera.app)

### Inferences
- Banks address "parent controls child's spending"; Büdcəm and Cashera address "household sees and plans shared money". Nobody found offers a cross-bank family view except via manual/notification entry.
- Büdcəm Family (~5 ₼/month equivalent) undercuts Cashera Plus Family (7.99 ₼).

### Gaps
- Birbank/Kapital Bank, Unibank (outside Leobank), PASHA Bank, m10 family features: not found.
- Other local budgeting apps with family modes: none beyond Cashera found.
