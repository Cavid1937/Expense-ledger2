# Direct competitors: Büdcəm and Cashera (8 Nov 2026) - nothing built

Evidence labels: SITE = read from the company's own page today; OWNER = screenshot/video/text supplied by the owner; OPINION = my judgement. Neither app was installed or used by me.

## Cashera (cashera.app, ProTech Solutions Group MMC, "born in Azerbaijan")
- SITE: iPhone + Android + web ERP; 7 languages (az, en, ru, tr, de, uz, kk); manat default; widgets on both platforms; data syncs across devices (so it is server-based, unlike us).
- SITE features: chat-style logging ("type it in plain words", several items per message), voice input, receipt photo scan, wallets, payment calendar with reminders, recurring-payment suggestions, savings goals, groups/split, a GIFT LEDGER (weddings, birthdays - very Azerbaijani), debts both ways incl. instalments, statement import PDF/CSV/Excel with duplicate detection and review, category learning from corrections, undo.
- SITE auto-tracking: Android reads bank NOTIFICATIONS (owner's screenshot confirms the Android "Notification read, reply & control" permission screen for "Cashera avtomatik xərc qeydi"); iOS via Shortcuts; user confirms each captured entry; can be switched off; no SMS mentioned; does NOT connect to bank accounts. Banks are not named. => the notification route we assessed is what the main local competitor already ships.
- SITE pricing (AZN, bank card in Azerbaijan): Free 0 (10 AI questions/day, 5 scans/month); Plus 4.99/month or 39/year (14-day trial); Plus Family 7.99/month or 59/year (2-5 people); Business 19.99/month or 189/year (POS, invoices, warehouse, ERP); Avtoyuma add-on 5/month.
- SITE privacy claims: encrypted in transit and at rest on the server, no card data, app lock (Face ID/fingerprint/PIN), hide amounts in notifications, CSV export, account deletion removes server data.

## Büdcəm (budcem.az, Kodivo Tech MMC)
- SITE: iOS + Android; Azerbaijani site; AI advisor (Google Gemini) for spending analysis, month forecast, savings tips, Q&A over your own data; daily limit alerts ("how much to cut tomorrow"); family budget (owner + up to 4 members, permissions, shared dashboard); savings goals with daily target; loans and subscriptions; mood tag on entries; onboarding questionnaire that produces an AI budget plan; partner discounts (Padel Point 50%, Hygge 20%, FitStop 10%) for paid users.
- SITE pricing (USD, billed through Apple/Google): Personal $1.99/month, Family $2.99/month, 7-day trial; annual cheaper, price not shown.
- OWNER (terms + privacy text, updated 16 Oct 2026): phone-number login with one-time SMS code, Apple/Google sign-in; no passwords. Data is stored on their servers. Receipt and debt photo OCR and text entry go to Anthropic's API (30-day retention noted), analysis to Google Gemini; photo OCR needs explicit consent that can be withdrawn. PRIVACY TEXT SAYS bank-notification content is processed ON THEIR SERVER when the user enables it (extracted: amount, currency, merchant, card last digits, balance); original notification text not kept in logs. Telegram AI feature exists. Servers may be outside Azerbaijan. Not for under-13. Subscription is not cancelled by deleting the account.
- OWNER video (about 70 s, Büdcəm first run, 9 steps): intro "Büdcəniz üçün ağıllı nəzarət"; step "How does your money come?" (fixed monthly / variable-irregular income); salary day picker 1-31 ("forecasts adapt to your pay cycle"); "Do you have a bank card?" with a card-balance add (balance added to the total); "Do you have a credit debt right now?" (no / yes); final "What is your main goal?" (keep spending under control / save). Clean light cards, green accent, one question per screen, progress dots "ADDIM 4/9".
- SITE does not mention bank notifications on the marketing page, but the privacy text does, so the feature exists or is planned (unverified which).

## What this means for us (OPINION unless marked)
1. We are NOT alone: two local apps already do AI chat entry, receipts, family sharing and (Cashera confirmed, Büdcəm implied) bank-notification capture. Our differences that are real today: everything stays on the phone (no server, no phone-number account, no AI vendor receiving financial data), Safe to Spend maths with float/pace/run-out lines, offline, free. These are privacy-first strengths, but only matter if users value them; unvalidated.
2. Notification capture moves from "nice idea" to "expected feature" in this market. Our design (allow-listed bank package, parsed locally, review list, balance-chain check) is a privacy-better version of what both do. Still needs owner go-ahead and bank samples (Birbank pending).
3. Gaps they cover that we lack: onboarding questionnaire (income type, pay day, cards, credit debt, goal) - cheap, high value, local, no AI needed; gift ledger (weddings) - culturally specific, small; chat/voice entry (local parsing possible, no server); payment calendar (we have bills); family/shared groups (needs sync first); web/ERP business side (out of scope).
4. Pricing anchors: Cashera Plus 4.99 AZN/month, 39/year; Büdcəm about $2/month. A free, local-only app can use "no subscription" as a message; monetisation is a later decision.
5. Their privacy exposure is our opening: both hold users' financial data on servers and send some to US AI vendors. A "your data never leaves the phone" claim is checkable in our code (no network calls) - keep it true.
6. Suggested order if the owner agrees (not started): onboarding questionnaire; local sentence entry ("bolt 12"); gift ledger; notification review list (after samples); sync only with end-to-end encryption.

Open questions: real-phone behaviour of both apps (I did not run them); whether Cashera lists supported banks inside the app; exact Büdcəm annual price; actual user numbers (neither site states any).
