# Azerbaijan competitors for a business (microbusiness) bookkeeping mode

Access date for all fetches: 28 Oct 2026. Tags: FACT (official/primary), VENDOR CLAIM, REVIEW (user evidence), INFERENCE. Hands-on testing of any product: NOT ASSESSED (none done). Fetches were summarised by a fetch tool, not read raw; treat exact wording as paraphrase.

## 1. Cashera (re-check of listing, version history, Business plan)

### Takeaway
Cashera is the only Azerbaijan-focused app found that bundles personal finance with a broad, fast-built business suite (cash boxes, invoices, POS, warehouse, roles, partners, web ERP) at 19.99 AZN/month. Almost all business features shipped in about 8 weeks (Aug-Oct), and the independent evidence is tiny (10 ratings). It is a real competitor in positioning but its maturity is unproven.

### Cited Findings
- Listing: "Cashera: AI Expense Tracker", developer PROTECH SOLUTIONS GROUP, MMC; Finance; 46.7 MB; iOS 16+; 7 languages (EN, AZ, DE, KK, RU, TR, UZ); 37 currencies — FACT (store listing) [App Store AZ](https://apps.apple.com/az/app/cashera-ai-expense-tracker/id6788484318), Apple, accessed 28 Oct 2026.
- Rating 4.9 from 10 ratings; 4 review texts shown, all short and positive; one reviewer (Nazlimmdv, 10 Sept) identifies as an accountant who finds it useful for personal and business finances; the developer replied to each review — REVIEW, same source. Year of review dates not shown by the page; assumed 2026. Four visible reviews are too few to indicate demand and developer replies suggest possible early-network/friendly reviewers (INFERENCE).
- Version history (day/month only on the page; year not displayed; all dates likely 2026 given the app id and "2.0.x" lineage — INFERENCE), FACT (release notes) same App Store source:
  - 2.0.54 21 Aug in-app payment page; 2.0.56 24 Aug separation of personal and business accounts; 2.0.57 9 Sept instant sync.
  - 2.0.65 17 Sept company mode fixes, server-side totals, partner ownership and cash box transfers; 2.0.66 19 Sept card-to-cash-box mapping.
  - 2.0.68 21 Sept full support for 7 languages; 2.0.71 26 Sept offline expense logging with sync.
  - 2.0.72 30 Sept plans renamed Free/Plus/Pro/Business; 2.0.73 1 Oct business reports, transaction filters, accountant role.
  - 2.0.75 3 Oct business sales, products, customers, warehouse; 2.0.76 4 Oct PDF invoices, customer portal, seller and accountant roles, supplier accounts; 2.0.78 6 Oct simpler business transaction form.
  - 2.0.79 9 Oct business categories by company type; Plus and Pro subscriptions via App Store.
  - 2.0.84 11 Oct no-signup start, offline chat; 2.0.85 14 Oct new look, payment calendar, savings goals, bank statement import, business POS, family budget.
  - 2.0.90 16 Oct widgets, family/group sync; 2.0.92 17 Oct business boxes, queue, orders, loyalty, car wash commission; 2.0.95 19 Oct POS fix, cash till for new businesses; 2.0.96 20 Oct payment fixes.
- Genuinely new vs earlier: earliest company-mode traces are 17 Sept (fixes imply it existed before) with business/personal separation 24 Aug; invoices/warehouse/roles/POS appeared 3-14 Oct; cash boxes/till/orders/loyalty 17-19 Oct. About 15 releases in 2 months, suggesting very high churn (INFERENCE; also a stability risk: repeated "POS fix", "company mode fixes").
- Website claims, VENDOR CLAIM [cashera.app](https://cashera.app/), accessed 28 Oct 2026: business mode turns personal panel into company panel; kassa (cash/bank desks, shift closing); customer invoices (qaimələr) with balances, paid invoices post to cash register; POS with barcode, receipts, cash/card/credit (nisyə); web ERP (cash, warehouse, reservations, price lists, attendance, scales, valuation); Business plan also lists reports, payroll, partners without detail; roles: cashier sees cash register only, accountant sees reports, owner sees all; each operation records who did it.
- Pricing, VENDOR CLAIM same website (AZ prices, bank card): Free 0; Plus 4.99 AZN/mo or 39/yr (14-day trial); Plus Family 7.99/mo or 59/yr; Business 19.99 AZN/mo or 189/yr (unlimited companies, POS, invoices, warehouse, web ERP, team roles); Avtoyuma add-on 5 AZN/mo. The App Store page lists no prices (FACT, App Store).
- Sync/offline: site says account shared across devices and syncs; site does not mention offline use (VENDOR CLAIM). App Store notes mention offline expense logging with sync (2.0.71) and offline chat (2.0.84), so offline appears limited to expense entry, not demonstrated for business records (INFERENCE). Cloud account model: server-side storage claimed encrypted; CSV export of all data claimed; account deletion removes server data (VENDOR CLAIM).
- Privacy label (developer-declared, unverified by Apple): linked to user — other financial info, email, name, phone, photos/videos, user ID, device ID, crash/performance data; all for App Functionality — FACT (label), App Store source.
- Contrast with Expense Ledger: Cashera is server-synced/account-based; local-first is a differentiator (INFERENCE).

### Inferences
- Cashera sets the feature bar an AZ buyer sees for "personal + business in one app", and shows a price anchor: 19.99 AZN/mo for business, 4.99 for personal premium.
- Breadth in 8 weeks with 10 ratings suggests broad but shallow coverage; accounting correctness (partial payments, reconciliation) unverified.

## 3. Local business software and services (substitute vs complement)

### Takeaway
Real small-business bookkeeping in Azerbaijan is shaped by mandatory state systems (e-qaimə, e-kassa, e-Mühasibim) and bank business apps, which are complements, not substitutes. Full ERP/POS vendors cover retail/restaurants with no public pricing.

### Cited Findings
- SOFTECH: ~20 modules (Retail & Market with e-kassa integration, Simple POS with fiscal integration, Warehouse & Trade with supplier/payables, Mobile Sales with offline order taking, HR & Payroll, Financial Management, double-entry Accounting & Audit with automated tax reports, Loan & Pawnshop, etc.). No prices; "Call Now" — VENDOR CLAIM [SOFTECH products](https://www.softech.az/en/products), accessed 28 Oct 2026. Role: heavy ERP/POS; competes with Cashera Business's POS/warehouse side, not with a simple ledger. Weakness: no public price, sales-contact model (INFERENCE: aimed at established businesses).
- e-qaimə-faktura (State Tax Service): electronic invoices mandatory since 01.04.2017 for VAT-registered persons listed in Tax Code Art. 218.1.2; issued online via e-taxes.gov.az with ASAN İmza/e-signature, or offline via eFP program and uploaded; offline method mainly for large taxpayers; page states no fee — FACT [taxes.gov.az](https://www.taxes.gov.az/az/page/elektron-qaime-faktura). Role: legal invoicing; a simple ledger cannot replace it; a complement (export/reconcile) at best (INFERENCE).
- 2025 Cabinet regulation created "e-mühasibat" system (Ministry of Finance) for electronic financial reports and covering accounting software requirements — per search summary, primary regulation not read; verify at [meclis.gov.az](https://meclis.gov.az/documents/1059-VIQD.pdf) (listed in search, not opened) — treat as unverified.
- Birbank Biznes (Kapital Bank): free iPhone app, EN/AZ/RU; account statements up to 1 year, QR cash-out, international transfers, FX, utility/budget payments, pay by link/QR, templates, salary receipt, business cards; rating 4.9 from ~5.7k; release notes through 3.5.4 only "minor bug fixes"; reviews: login problem despite Face ID, dark mode request — FACT/REVIEW [App Store AZ](https://apps.apple.com/az/app/birbank-biznes/id1546071871). Order-payment feature since Aug 2024 and micro-business loan promotions (7 Aug 2026) per [Trend](https://www.trend.az/azerbaijan/business/3936416.html) / [Trend](https://www.trend.az/business/4213421.html) (search snippets, not opened).
- ABB Biz (International Bank of Azerbaijan): free; for individual entrepreneurs/small business; onboarding, digital loans, mobile POS ordering, AniPay QR payments, simplified tax declarations and payments via "E-Mühasibim", transfers, budget/utility payments, assigned manager; 4.8 from 24 ratings; v5.24.0 (2 Oct) added open banking in loan applications, POS ordering; one review (Jamila, 10/06/2025) says support/account opening impossible — VENDOR CLAIM/REVIEW [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1593388351). Notably E-Mühasibim is a tax-filing bridge that overlaps the "accountant/tax" job (INFERENCE).
- Not researched (budget exhausted): 1C partners and prices, PASHA Bank business app (only a search snippet mentioning "Payment via Link", "Online Business Loan"), m10/Millikart terminals, Kapital Bank, e-kassa app specifics, Telegram/WhatsApp/Excel substitutes, media pricing news (report.az, trend.az, apa.az, bizim.media, marja.az).

### Inferences
- Banks give payments, statements and credit but not bookkeeping with customer/supplier balances; state systems cover compliance; ERPs cover retail. A light ledger would sit alongside these (complement), with the main uncontested niche being owner-operator tracking for people who do not need ERP.
- "Substitute" threats for a ledger: spreadsheets/Telegram notes (no evidence collected), Cashera, and bank statement exports.

### Gaps
- 1C, e-kassa/POS app pricing and adoption among microbusinesses: no data found.
- Share of individual entrepreneurs using accounting software at all: no data found.

## 4. Gaps in under-served workflows

### Takeaway
No independent review evidence was found demonstrating unmet needs; the only evidence is indirect (competitor feature emphasis). The conclusion "do not build" cannot be ruled out or supported by this research.

### Cited Findings
- Cashera prioritising invoices with outstanding balances, supplier accounts, customer portal and reconciliation statements, partner contributions, nisyə (credit) POS payments, suggests competitors treat customer/supplier balances and owner/partner money as core — VENDOR CLAIM / release notes, [Cashera](https://cashera.app/), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/az/app/cashera-ai-expense-tracker/id6788484318).
- hev.money says multi-user/corporate needs are done as custom work, i.e. not served by its free product — VENDOR CLAIM [hev.az](https://hev.az/en/maliyye-proqrami/).
- Bank business apps stress payments, loans and tax filing rather than bookkeeping (feature lists above); AZ/RU/EN UI is provided by banks and Cashera (languages listed above), so language alone is not a gap for these.
- One reviewer calls Cashera useful for personal and business finances as an accountant (Nazlimmdv, 10 Sept, [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/az/app/cashera-ai-expense-tracker/id6788484318)) — one data point.
- Complaints visible: bank app login/support (Birbank Biznes review; ABB Biz review Jamila 10/06/2025) — these concern bank services, not bookkeeping.

### Inferences
- Partial payments, customer/supplier balances, owner money and accountant export appear to be served at least on paper by Cashera; under-service, if it exists, is likely in trust/maturity (10 ratings), cloud-vs-local privacy, offline business use and accountant-ready export — hypotheses only, need user interviews.
- Local-first is a possible differentiator against Cashera's server-based, account-linked data (privacy label lists name, phone, email, photos), but whether AZ microbusinesses value this is unvalidated.

### Gaps
- No independent AZ reviews/forum/Telegram evidence of pain points was found; Google Play reviews not accessed; no interviews. Recommend lightweight validation with 5-10 individual entrepreneurs before building.
