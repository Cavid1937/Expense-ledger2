# Azerbaijan household-finance context for a financial coach (as of October 2026)

Access date for all URLs below: 2026-10-30. Labels: PRIMARY (official body or its own publication, as reported), SECONDARY (news/vendor/aggregator relaying official data), INFERENCE (mine). Method note: web search returns summaries; several page fetches (stat.gov.az, meydan.tv, oc-media.org) failed with ECONNRESET, so stat.gov.az household-budget-survey tables were NOT read directly. Most "PRIMARY" data below is the State Statistics Committee / CBAR data as relayed by Trend, Report, Interfax, so strictly SECONDARY in access path.

## 1. Income and expense structure (budget shares, wages, informality, pay timing, seasonality, remittances)

### Takeaway
Food is the dominant and fastest-inflating budget item (official food/alcohol/tobacco inflation 6.9% y/y in Sept 2026 versus 3.6% non-food), average pay is about 1,175 AZN against a 400 AZN minimum wage, and a large, officially unmeasured informal sector plus rising remittances mean many households have irregular, partly cash income. I could not retrieve an official COICOP household-budget-survey breakdown (housing, transport, communication, health, education shares), and found nothing sourced on avans, pay dates, Novruz or school-year costs.

### Cited Findings
- Minimum wage is 400 AZN/month in 2026, unchanged from 2025 (about 36% of average gross salary per Wage.is) — SECONDARY: [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/azerbaijan/minimum-wages), [Report.az](https://report.az/en/finance/minimum-wage-in-azerbaijan-may-remain-at-azn400-in-2026)
- Average monthly nominal wage of hired employees: 1,174 AZN in Jan-Jun 2026 (+7% y/y), 1,177 AZN in Jan-Jul 2026 (+7.2%), per State Statistics Committee as relayed by Report.az — SECONDARY (of PRIMARY): [Report.az](https://report.az/en/finance/average-monthly-salary-in-azerbaijan-rises-7). Lower aggregator figures (about 980-1,100 AZN) exist and are likely older/different definitions: [Wage.is](https://wage.is/azerbaijan/), [Hellopebl](https://hellopebl.com/resources/blog/average-salary-in-azerbaijan)
- Food share: a Meydan.tv article headline states 56 of every 100 manat go to food; snippet says 56.10 AZN groceries and tobacco, 16.70 non-grocery, 13.40 clothing, smaller shares fuel, household goods, pharmaceuticals, technology, for 2023. Page fetch failed; the category list looks like retail-trade turnover structure rather than a household budget survey, so do NOT treat 56% as the household budget share — SECONDARY, UNVERIFIED: [Meydan.tv](https://www.meydan.tv/en/article/azerbaycanda-qazanilan-her-100-manatin-56-manati-erzaga-gedir/)
- JAM News (21 Oct 2026) uses illustrative families spending 60%, 40%, 25% of income on food to show that identical food inflation hits low-income families harder; explicitly hypothetical, no national data — SECONDARY: [JAM News](https://jam-news.net/?p=237098)
- 2015 household survey (1,200 household heads, late 2015, after the devaluation): 80% said income was not enough to cover household expenses; financial literacy higher among men — SECONDARY (survey summary): [Search summary of the CBAR/World Bank-linked literacy surveys, see also World Bank microdata](https://microdata.worldbank.org/catalog/1024)
- Remittances: individual transfers into Azerbaijan $748.5m in H1 2026 (+40.2% y/y), Russia 47.7% ($356.6m), Turkey $108.3m; outflows $207.9m (-12.7%); 2025 full-year inflows about $1.18bn (+8.7%) — SECONDARY (CBAR data): [Trend](https://www.trend.az/business/4223992.html), [Report.az](https://report.az/en/finance/cba-reveals-2025-money-transfer-figures-in-azerbaijan), [Azertag](https://azertag.az/en/xeber/foreign_remittances_to_azerbaijan_exceed_748_million_in_first_six_months-4413744)
- Informality: no official recent estimate of informal employment published for about 7 years; the 2018 Employment Law defines it — SECONDARY: [Baku Research Institute](https://bakuresearchinstitute.org/en/why-does-azerbaijan-not-disclose-the-total-number-of-informal-jobs/)
- Labour Code backstop on late pay: employer owes compensation of 1% of wages per day of delay (reported via WageIndicator, not checked against the Code text); wage-arrears cases documented in media/construction/TV sectors, mostly 2012-2022 — SECONDARY: [WageIndicator](https://wageindicator.org/labour-laws/labour-law-around-the-world/minimum-wages-regulations/minimum-wages-regulations-azerbaijan), [Meydan.tv](https://www.meydan.tv/en/?p=180618)

### Inferences
- INFERENCE: With average wage about 3x the minimum wage and a distribution not published in my sources, "average" income is a poor default for coach thresholds; the coach should use the user's own recorded income.
- INFERENCE: Because food inflation exceeds general inflation and food share is high for lower-income households, "your food spending rose X%" needs a price-vs-volume caveat (prices rose about 7%).
- INFERENCE: Remittances from Russia nearly half of inflows suggests some households receive irregular foreign-currency transfers; the app should treat transfers as a distinct, irregular income type.

### Gaps
- Official household budget survey (stat.gov.az) shares for housing/utilities, transport, communication, health, education: not retrieved (site fetch failed).
- Salary payment timing (monthly vs avans/two-part pay), typical payday, delays in the private sector: no reliable source found; practice assumed from general knowledge only, do not state as fact.
- Seasonal costs (Novruz in March, school year in September, weddings, heating season): no sourced figures found.
- Second jobs/freelance/irregular income prevalence: no source found.

## 2. Prices, currency, dollarization, deposits and insurance

### Takeaway
Inflation re-accelerated in 2025-26 (5.5% y/y in Sept 2026, food 6.9%), the AZN was cut twice in 2015 (Feb and Dec) and dollar loans hurt borrowers, yet deposit dollarization has fallen to 34% (households about 25.6% in June 2026). Deposits are insured up to 100,000 AZN per bank, but only if the rate is under the Fund's caps.

### Cited Findings
- Sept 2026 CPI (State Statistics Committee, 12 Oct 2026): overall +0.9% m/m, +5.5% y/y, Jan-Sep +5.7%; food/alcohol/tobacco +1.4% m/m, +6.9% y/y; non-food +0.4% m/m, +3.6% y/y; paid services +0.6% m/m, +5.1% y/y — SECONDARY (of PRIMARY): [Trend](https://www.trend.az/business/4233786.html); Interfax gives a different Jan-Sep cumulative (3.7%) so that figure is contested: [Interfax](https://interfax.com/newsroom/top-stories/119581/)
- Vegetables had the sharpest monthly rise in Sept; Economy Ministry raised its 2026 average inflation forecast to 5.7% from 4.8%, citing food prices and regulated tariff changes — SECONDARY: [Tradingeconomics summary](https://tradingeconomics.com/azerbaijan/inflation-rate-mom/news/591568), [Report.az](https://report.az/en/amp/finance/azerbaijan-updates-average-annual-inflation-forecast)
- 2025 average inflation 5.6% (2.2% in 2024); food inflation 6.8% (from 1.3%), services 6.5% — SECONDARY (ADB Asian Development Outlook April 2026): [ADB](https://www.adb.org/sites/default/files/publication/1135881/aze-ado-april-2026.pdf)
- 2015: the manat lost about a third in Feb 2015 (reports vary 25-35%) and the CBAR floated the currency on 21 Dec 2015 with a further large fall (reports vary 32-48%); share of USD loans rose from 26% to 44% within a year to Sept 2015; Constitutional Court required restructuring of FX loans on request, and CBAR regulations gave compensation for the manat rise in principal — SECONDARY: [ADB TCR](https://www.adb.org/sites/default/files/project-documents/48917/48917-001-tcr-en.pdf), [RFE/RL](https://www.rferl.org/a/azerbaijan-suffers-currency-crash-oil-prices/27479606.html), [Intellinews](https://www.intellinews.com/azerbaijan-floats-the-manat-87222/). I did not retrieve information on the 2016 episode or the later peg to the USD at about 1.70; do not state without checking.
- Dollarization (CBAR Financial Stability Report H1 2026): bank deposit dollarization 34% (-1.6 pp in H1), loan dollarization 15.6% (+1.4 pp), both near historical lows — SECONDARY: [Trend 13 Oct 2026](https://www.trend.az/azerbaijan/4234379.html)
- Resident individuals' deposit dollarization 25.6% at end-June 2026 (-3.8 pp since start of year); retail time-deposit dollarization 28.5% — SECONDARY: [Trend](https://www.trend.az/business/4234382.html)
- Deposits end-H1 2026: AZN 26.2bn in manat, AZN 13.2bn in FX — SECONDARY: [Trend](https://www.trend.az/business/4214559.html)
- CBAR refinancing rate cut to 6.5% from 5 Feb 2026; rates on new AZN deposits and loans have declined; legal-entity AZN deposit rate 6.4% (June 2026); I found no confirmed 2026 average household deposit rate for AZN or USD — SECONDARY: [CBAR press release](https://www.cbar.az/press-release-5417/central-bank-cuts-refinancing-rate-and-other-interest-rate-corridor-parameters-by-025-pp), [Trend](https://www.trend.az/business/4214559.html)
- Deposit insurance (Azerbaijan Deposit Insurance Fund, ADIF/Əmanətlərin Sığortalanması Fondu): compensation up to 100,000 AZN per depositor per participating bank (bank page says since April 2021); only deposits at or below Fund-set max rates (12% AZN, 2.5% FX per a Trend piece on a bank closure) are covered; paid in deposit currency; written application required — SECONDARY: [ABB Bank](https://abb-bank.az/en/ferdi/emanetler/emanetin-sigortalanmasi), [Trend](https://www.trend.az/business/3815016.html), [Trend](https://www.trend.az/business/3348333.html). Older sources show 30,000 AZN; verify on ADIF website before the app states a number.

### Inferences
- INFERENCE: Many households keep USD informally (cash, not only bank deposits); official share covers bank deposits only. Savings-in-USD advice should be framed as "currency-risk diversification", not a recommendation, and the coach should not assert that the AZN will or will not move.
- INFERENCE: Because the 2015 shocks are within living memory, "keep part of savings in USD" is culturally familiar; however the recent trend (falling dollarization, AZN deposit rates comparable with or above FX) suggests the coach should present both options neutrally.
- INFERENCE: Deposit-insurance coverage limits make "spread large deposits across banks" a valid generic tip, but only after confirming the current limit.

### Gaps
- Current AZN vs USD retail deposit rates (2026): not found reliably.
- 2016 devaluation details and current exchange-rate regime wording: not retrieved.
- Official 2025-26 ADIF page not fetched; limit date and rate caps unverified.
- Share of cash USD/EUR savings held outside banks: no source found.

## 3. Financial literacy and behaviour (surveys, cards, mobile payments, trust, apps)

### Takeaway
Hard recent behavioural data are thin in what I could retrieve: the 2025 Global Findex (2024 survey, Azerbaijan n=1,000) exists but I could not access Azerbaijan headline numbers, and older literacy surveys (OECD 2017, CBAR/World Bank 2009 and 2015-16) show low financial knowledge. Card use has grown strongly (cash withdrawals share down from 87% to 43% of card flows, 2017-2023).

### Cited Findings
- Global Findex 2025 includes an Azerbaijan 2024 wave (1,000 respondents); headline Azerbaijan figures not retrieved — SECONDARY: [World Bank microdata](https://microdata.worldbank.org/catalog/7867/data-dictionary)
- Older World Bank-linked figure: 36% of adults with a bank account in the 2016 report (survey of 4,620 adults); IFC reports a 29% account rate pre-2025 — SECONDARY and dated: [Report.az](https://report.az/en/finance/36-of-adults-in-azerbaijan-have-a-bank-account), [World Bank 2016](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2016/05/24/more-reforms-needed-to-improve-financial-capability-and-inclusion-in-azerbaijan)
- OECD/INFE 2017 survey: average literacy score 11 of 21; fewer than one in four adults reached the minimum target of 4 of 7 on knowledge — SECONDARY: [OECD](https://search.oecd.org/financial/education/financial-education-in-azerbaijan.htm)
- Mastercard-CBAR study (Feb 2026): card transaction value rose sixfold and the share of cash withdrawals fell from 87% to 43% over 2017-2023; CBAR strategy 2024-2026 targets over 40% cashless share in retail trade — SECONDARY: [BIIA](https://www.biia.com/mastercard-and-the-central-bank-of-azerbaijan-unveil-joint-study-the-next-wave-of-digitization-in-azerbaijan/)
- Cashless payments April 2026: 9.09bn AZN (-2.2% y/y); 2024 domestic card payments 78.9bn AZN — SECONDARY: [Report.az](https://report.az/en/amp/finance/cashless-payments-in-azerbaijan-down-2-2-in-april), CBAR digital payments review: [CBAR](https://uploads.cbar.az/assets/Digital%20payments%20review_2025_KR_MI_final.pdf) (not opened)
- ADB projection: about 3.85 million people (about a third of population) using digital payments by 2025 — SECONDARY: [APA](https://en.apa.az/finance/adb-15-million-people-in-azerbaijan-have-started-using-financial-system-in-recent-years-466882)
- Household debt reached 18.7% of disposable income (+0.3 pp in H1 2026); 37% of consumer loan book has debt-to-income above 45%; NPL ratio 3%, NPLs +25% in H1; overdue loans 719.4m AZN (2.1%) on 1 July 2026 — SECONDARY (CBAR FSR via Trend/Report): [Trend](https://www.trend.az/azerbaijan/4234373.html), [Trend](https://www.trend.az/business/4234370.html), [Report.az](https://report.az/en/amp/finance/overdue-loans-exceed-719m-manats-in-azerbaijan). JAM News links arrears to weak real income growth and new loans used to repay old: [JAM News](https://jam-news.net/why-bad-loans-are-rising-in-azerbaijan/)

### Inferences
- INFERENCE: Coach copy should explain basic terms (income vs balance vs available money) plainly; literacy surveys, though old, point that way.
- INFERENCE: A debt-to-income nudge at about 45% matches the regulator's own risk benchmark, a locally defensible threshold (verify the exact regulatory definition before using).

### Gaps
- Findex 2025 Azerbaijan: account ownership, saving, borrowing, reasons for not saving, digital payment use: not retrieved.
- Trust in banks, Telegram usage for money, e-wallet and app popularity (e.g. banking apps, Kapital Bank Birbank, ABB mobile, m10, Azericard): no sourced data found. Do not name apps as "common" without a source.
- Share of people who budget: not found.

## 4. Utilities and recurring bills

### Takeaway
Electricity and gas tariffs are being rebalanced from 2026 (new fixed monthly charges of 1 AZN each for households plus phased increases), so utility bills are rising by regulation; no sourced details on water, internet, mobile, rent or BakiKart billing patterns were found.

### Cited Findings
- From 1 Jan 2026 a fixed monthly tariff of 1 AZN applies for household electricity (single-phase) and natural gas, intended to spread fixed costs — SECONDARY: [Report.az](https://report.az/en/amp/energy/azerbaijan-sets-fixed-tariffs-for-electricity-and-natural-gas)
- One analysis says increases of 5-15% depending on usage, average household electricity tariff up about 7.8%, full implementation by 2028; JAM News earlier reported gas average tariff +8.7% and electricity +7.8% in an earlier year round (year unconfirmed) — SECONDARY, date uncertain: [JAM News](https://jam-news.net/wages-rise-in-azerbaijan-but-so-do-utility-costs/)
- Economy Ministry cited "regulated tariff changes" as a driver of the raised 2026 inflation forecast — SECONDARY: [Report.az](https://report.az/en/amp/finance/azerbaijan-updates-average-annual-inflation-forecast)
- Official electricity tariff table (AzerIsiq) on the open-data portal; not opened — [opendata.az](https://opendata.az/en/azerisiq-asc/elektrik-enerjisi-tarifleri)

### Inferences
- INFERENCE (general knowledge, unsourced): typical recurring list for a coach to pre-suggest is electricity (isiq), gas (qaz), water (su), internet, mobile top-up, rent (icare), loan instalments (kredit/taksit), transport top-up (BakiKart), plus possibly kommunal/garbage fees. Billing patterns (prepaid meters, monthly postpaid, pay-via-terminal) must be confirmed with local users.
- INFERENCE: Gas/heating bills are seasonal (winter), so a coach should compare month to the same month last year and spread annual cost, rather than flag winter as overspending.

### Gaps
- Water, internet, mobile operator tariffs; rent levels; BakiKart behaviour; prepaid vs postpaid share: not found.
- Heating-season dates and size of the bill: not found.

## 5. Language, tone and cultural sensitivity

### Takeaway
I found no sourced style guide for Azerbaijani or Russian financial messaging; everything below is INFERENCE from general language knowledge and should be validated by native-speaking reviewers.

### Cited Findings
- None sourced. (Searches did not target this and returned no usable linguistic or cultural-research sources; claims about informal vs formal address and terms are not backed by a citation.)

### Inferences
- INFERENCE: Use polite "siz" form (Azerbaijani) and "vy" (Russian) with adults; avoid slang.
- INFERENCE: Common everyday terms likely to be understood: budget (budce), qənaət / əmanət (saving / deposit), borc (debt), kredit (loan), taksit (instalment), avans (advance pay), maaş (salary), kommunal (utility bills), xərc (expense), gəlir (income). In Russian: бюджет, сбережения, долг, кредит, рассрочка, аванс, зарплата, коммуналка. Verify each with native speakers.
- INFERENCE: Debt and money talk can be sensitive; avoid shaming wording ("you overspent") and avoid advising to cut family support or gifts, which are often social obligations. Frame as "planned" vs "unplanned" instead.
- INFERENCE: Do not imply a single "household" holds all money; incomes and obligations to extended family (parents, relatives abroad) are common (supported partly by large inbound remittances).

### Gaps
- No native review, no user research, no sources on register preference by age; all language claims are unvalidated.

## 6. Tax and benefit facts affecting a personal budget (high level, 2026)

### Takeaway
From 1 Jan 2026 the seven-year income-tax holiday for non-oil private-sector employees ended: monthly income up to 2,500 AZN is now taxed at 3% (5% in 2027, 7% from 2028) and medical insurance now applies above 2,500 AZN. Official State Tax Service text was not retrieved; PwC (reviewed 28 Sept 2026) is the best available source.

### Cited Findings
- Income tax for non-oil private-sector employees from 1 Jan 2026: up to 2,500 AZN/month 3%; 2,501-8,000 AZN: 75 AZN plus 10% of the excess over 2,500; above 8,000: 625 AZN plus 14% of the excess; lowest bracket rises to 5% in 2027 and 7% from 2028. Previously 0% up to 8,000 AZN — SECONDARY: [PwC Tax Summaries](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/azerbaijan/individual/significant-developments); consistent with [OC Media summary](https://oc-media.org/running-out-of-time-azerbaijan-looks-to-tax-hikes-to-counter-shrinking-hydrocarbon-reserves/) (page fetch failed; snippet only)
- Mandatory state social insurance up to 8,000 AZN unchanged: employee 3% on first 200 AZN and 10% above; employer 22% on first 200 and 15% above; above 8,000 combined falls 25% to 21% — SECONDARY: [PwC](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/azerbaijan/individual/significant-developments) (a Mercans PDF and other vendors differ in wording: [Mercans](https://mercans.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Azerbaijan_Changes-in-Tax-rate-and-social-Security-Rates_Jan-2026.pdf))
- Mandatory health insurance: threshold dropped from 8,000 to 2,500 AZN; above it the rate falls from 4% to 1%, shared equally; formula (salary - 2,500) x 0.5% + 50 AZN; unemployment insurance unchanged at 1% total — SECONDARY: [PwC](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/azerbaijan/individual/significant-developments)
- Other vendors report health insurance at 2% on income up to 2,500 and 0.5% above for both parties — conflicting with a simple reading of PwC; treat exact health-insurance numbers as unresolved: [Mercans EOR](https://mercans.com/employer-of-record/azerbaijan/)

### Inferences
- INFERENCE: Because net pay may have dropped at the start of 2026 for private-sector workers, coach rules based on "income compared with last year" may show an artificial decline in January 2026; advise users to enter net pay, and do not interpret the drop as a behavior change.
- INFERENCE: The coach should avoid computing tax for users; just say "check your payslip".

### Gaps
- State Tax Service (taxes.gov.az) official publication: not retrieved; public-sector, oil/gas, self-employed and simplified-tax rules not covered.
- Health-insurance wording conflict between vendors unresolved.

## 7. Implications for a coach rule (10-12 locally grounded ideas)

### Takeaway
Rules should lean on the user's own recorded data, use cautious non-prescriptive wording, and treat locally salient risks (food inflation, FX history, instalments, regulated utility rises, irregular transfers) as context, not as claims about the user.

### Cited Findings (rule ideas with evidence)
1. Irregular or split pay (avans/maaş): "Your income arrived on different dates this month; set your budget period from your last payday." Evidence: Labour Code late-pay compensation exists but I found no data on pay timing — evidence THIN; build as user-configurable, not as an assumption.
2. Food-price awareness: "Food prices were about 7% higher than a year ago (official, Sept 2026), so a rise in your food spending may be price, not extra buying." Evidence: [Trend](https://www.trend.az/business/4233786.html). Caution: update the figure from a data file, don't hard-code.
3. Food-share signal: if food exceeds a user-set share of income, show neutral info that lower-income households feel food inflation more. Evidence: [JAM News](https://jam-news.net/?p=237098) (illustrative); national share data not found, so do not benchmark users against an invented average.
4. Currency diversification, neutral: "Some people keep part of savings in more than one currency; the manat has changed value in the past (2015)." No forecasts. Evidence: [ADB](https://www.adb.org/sites/default/files/project-documents/48917/48917-001-tcr-en.pdf), dollarization data [Trend](https://www.trend.az/business/4234382.html). Caution: no advice to buy FX; the 25.6% household figure covers deposits only.
5. FX loan warning: if a loan is in a foreign currency while income is in AZN, show currency-risk note. Evidence: 2015 loan crisis and court-ordered restructuring ([RFE/RL](https://www.rferl.org/a/azerbaijan-suffers-currency-crash-oil-prices/27479606.html)); loan dollarization now 15.6% ([Trend](https://www.trend.az/azerbaijan/4234379.html)).
6. Instalments/kredit load: if monthly loan and taksit payments exceed about 45% of recorded income, show a caution. Evidence: CBAR flags 37% of consumer loans above 45% DTI ([Trend](https://www.trend.az/business/4234370.html)). Caution: confirm the regulator's exact definition; app sees only recorded data.
7. Debt spiral nudge: "A new loan used to repay another is a warning sign." Evidence: [JAM News](https://jam-news.net/why-bad-loans-are-rising-in-azerbaijan/) (commentary, secondary).
8. Utility seasonality and tariff change: compare gas/electricity with the same month last year; mention that regulated tariffs and new 1 AZN fixed charges apply from 2026. Evidence: [Report.az](https://report.az/en/amp/energy/azerbaijan-sets-fixed-tariffs-for-electricity-and-natural-gas). Caution: heating-season amounts not sourced; tariff schedule needs verifying.
9. Deposit insurance fact card: "Deposits at participating banks are insured up to 100,000 AZN (check ADIF site), if the interest rate is within the Fund's limits." Evidence: [ABB](https://abb-bank.az/en/ferdi/emanetler/emanetin-sigortalanmasi). Caution: older sources say 30,000; verify before shipping; not advice on where to deposit.
10. January 2026 take-home drop: if net income fell in Jan 2026, note that payroll tax rules changed (3% on income up to 2,500 AZN; health insurance applies above 2,500). Evidence: [PwC](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/azerbaijan/individual/significant-developments). Caution: vendors conflict on health-insurance details; do not calculate tax.
11. Remittance/transfer income: allow tagging a receipt as "transfer from relatives/abroad", exclude from "stable income" averages. Evidence: [Trend](https://www.trend.az/business/4223992.html). Caution: inferring dependence on remittances for a given user is not justified.
12. Family-support and cultural-event budgeting: suggest user-created "planned annual" envelopes (Novruz, school start, weddings, gifts) rather than judging them as waste. Evidence: NONE sourced (cultural reasoning only, INFERENCE); validate with user interviews before shipping.

### Inferences
- INFERENCE: Rules 1, 12 have the weakest evidence; rules 2, 6, 8, 9, 10 rest on current official or semi-official data that must be refreshed periodically and dated in the UI.
- INFERENCE: All wording needs native-speaker review in az/ru (see section 5); keep figures in editable data, not code.

### Gaps
- No user research, no verified payroll-timing data, no official budget-survey shares. Recommend a short validation with 5-10 Azerbaijani users on pay timing, USD savings habits and instalment use before finalizing rules 1, 4, 12.
