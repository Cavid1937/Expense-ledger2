# Poco F6 check list (for the owner, plain steps) — written 31 Oct 2026

Use the installed app. Tick what works, note what does not (a screenshot helps).

## App lock (More -> App lock)
1. Set a PIN (4-6 digits). Expected: "PIN saved", app stays open.
2. Swipe the app away from recent apps, open it again. Expected: lock screen asks for the PIN, nothing else visible.
3. Type a wrong PIN 5 times. Expected: a waiting message; closing and reopening does not reset the wait.
4. More -> App lock -> "Unlock with fingerprint" (appears only if the phone allows it). Expected: your phone's own fingerprint prompt opens, then the fingerprint button appears on the lock screen.
5. Lock the app, press the fingerprint button, use your finger. Expected: opens. Press it and cancel: expected "fingerprint did not work", and the PIN still works.
6. Lock screen -> "Forgot your PIN?" Expected: explanation, a 10-second wait, then "Remove the lock". Check your entries are all still there. (Only do this once you are ready to set a new PIN.)
7. Change "Lock the app" to 1 minute: leave the app for 2 minutes, come back. Expected: locked. Leave it for 20 seconds: expected not locked.

## Hide amounts
8. Tap the eye icon (amounts hidden). Swipe the app away from recent apps, reopen. Expected: still hidden. Turn it off, repeat: still shown.

## Icon
9. Look at the home-screen icon. Expected: a dark teal ledger page with a check mark. If you still see the old box, remove the app from the home screen and add it again (phones keep old icons until the app updates; this can take a day).

## Backup guide
10. More -> Keep your data safe. Expected: a section "Where do I find the backup file?". Make a backup, then find the file in Files -> Downloads. Tell me if the wording does not match what you see.

## Other new things
11. A loan with "annuity" repayment: type an extra amount under "What if I pay extra now?" Expected: months and interest saved.
12. Analytics -> Patterns: each line has a heading, "Based on N entries" and a Hide button.
