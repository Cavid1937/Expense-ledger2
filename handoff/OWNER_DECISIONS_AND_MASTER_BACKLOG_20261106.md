### D2. Android automatic backup (config change needs your approval; investigation does not)
**What the evidence says** ([Android Auto Backup documentation](https://developer.android.com/identity/data/autobackup), read today):
- The app manifest has `allowBackup="true"` and targets Android 16 (API 36). It has **no** backup rules, so by default Android backs up almost all of the app's private files to the user's Google Drive (up to 25 MB, one newest copy, encrypted end-to-end on Android 9+ when a screen lock exists, only if the user has Google backup switched on, roughly nightly on Wi-Fi when idle).
- It also allows direct phone-to-phone transfer at set-up.
- Restore happens when the app is installed (Play, set-up wizard, even `adb install`), before first launch.
- **Not confirmed:** whether the browser engine's stored website data (where this app's ledger lives) is inside what gets backed up. The documentation does not say; a third-party example includes that folder explicitly. I have **not** tested it. Status: UNCONFIRMED.
- With `allowBackup="false"` on Android 12+, some phone makers still do phone-to-phone transfer, so "off" may not mean fully off.

**Consequences:**
- *Keep it on (with accurate wording):* a lost or replaced phone may bring the ledger back automatically (a real benefit and free), but the ledger copy would sit in the user's Google account (encrypted), may be up to a day old, and "nothing leaves your phone" must be reworded. The PIN-lock record would come back too (the lock is not encryption).
- *Turn it off:* matches the current privacy sentence, no stale restores, but no automatic safety net; users depend on manual JSON backups, which are already the product's stated recovery path.
- *Middle path:* keep device-to-device transfer, allow cloud backup only when it can be encrypted, disclose both.

**Recommendation:** first let me **test** what is actually backed up and restored (Android's own `bmgr` tools on an emulator or your phone; read-only for the app, no config change), then choose. My expectation is that the middle path with disclosure is best for users; if the test shows partial or stale restores, choose "off".
**Blocked?** The manifest change and the final privacy sentence are blocked on you. The test is not.
