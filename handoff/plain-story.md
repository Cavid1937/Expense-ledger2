## 2. What each part is for, in one line (English; az and ru to follow the same sentences)

| Part | One line |
|---|---|
| Safe to Spend | What you can still spend this month, after your bills and savings. |
| Accounts | Where your money sits: cash, a card, a bank account. Each shows its balance. |
| Budgets | A limit you set for a kind of spending, so you see when you are close to it. |
| Goals | Something you are saving for, and how far you are. |
| Recurring payments | Bills and income that repeat, so the app counts them before they arrive. |
| Debts and loans | Money you owe and money owed to you, with what is left to pay. |
| Gifts | Who you gave to and who gave to you. |
| Groups (trips) | A label for entries that belong together, such as a holiday. |
| Spread the cost | Show one big payment over several months in charts and budgets. |
| Others will pay back | The part of a payment that is not yours, until they return it. |
| Another currency | Pay in dollars or euro; the app turns it into your currency at the official rate. |
| Import / Export | Save a copy of everything, move it to another phone, or bring in a bank statement. |
| App lock | A PIN or fingerprint so nobody else opens the app. |
| Coach | Short notes about your month: what changed and what to watch. |
