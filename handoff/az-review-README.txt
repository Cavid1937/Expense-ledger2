Expense Ledger — Azerbaijani wording review (for a native Azerbaijani speaker)

File: az-review-sheet.csv — opens in Excel or Google Sheets. One row per sentence the app can show (603 texts, 665 rows
including plural forms). Each row shows the Azerbaijani text with example values already filled in (amounts, counts,
dates, names), next to the English and Russian versions for context.

What to do, per row:
  1. Read the "Azerbaijani (rendered)" column as a user would see it in the app.
  2. In "Reviewer verdict" write OK, or "change".
  3. If "change": write your wording in "Suggested Azerbaijani wording". Keep every {placeholder} that appears in the
     "Placeholders" column (for example {amount}); the app fills those in.
  4. Use "Reviewer notes" for anything else (tone, term choice, a sentence that is correct but hard to understand).

Please pay special attention to:
  - money terms: spent vs planned/set aside, surplus vs deficit, target vs saved, recorded payment vs recurring bill;
  - comparison sentences (keys starting with cmp.) — they compare this month with the same days of last month;
  - goal and savings texts (goals.*, set.floor*, safe.*);
  - error, recovery and import messages (load.*, banner.*, import.*) — they must say what happened and what to do next.

Please do NOT change: amounts or the number format (1,234.56 — a separate owner decision), and the meaning of a sentence.

When finished, return the sheet with your name and the date. Until then the Azerbaijani wording is marked
IMPLEMENTED BUT UNVERIFIED (not reviewed by a native speaker).
