## 2. What changed, and why

1. **One line under Safe to Spend: "Until your next income on {date}: {amount} in your accounts after the payments due ({per day} a day)."**
   Why: Safe to Spend follows the calendar month. The day after pay day it can say 102 a day for 5 days while the salary has to last 29 days at 47 a day. The line shows the second figure beside the first. Safe to Spend itself is not changed.
   Shown only when accounts exist and the next income date is known. Silent otherwise.
2. **"Paid from" (the account chips) moved from "More details" into the main Add form.**
   Why: an entry with no account moves no balance. The chooser was folded away, and the very first entry has no "same as last time" to fall back on, so balances drifted from the start. This is the root of the Run 98 and 99 repairs.
   Cost: one more row in the form for people who use accounts. People without accounts see no change.
3. **Safe to Spend card names loan payments still due this month.**
   Finding (confirmed by reading the code and by a test): Safe to Spend takes out recurring payments, but never loan payments from the Debts page. With a 300 loan payment due on the 28th, the figure is 300 too high until that day.
   What I did: the card now says "Loan payments still due this month: X. They are not taken out of this figure, unless you also set them up as recurring payments."
   What I did not do: subtract them. That changes an established rule, and people who have the loan in both places would be counted twice. Owner decision D9.
4. **Fix of my own Run 99 defect:** in Azerbaijani and Russian "What if I buy something?" sat glued to "How is this calculated?" on one row. Found by screenshot. Own row now; regression check added.
5. **First launch:** the line under the app name kept changing language after a language was ticked. It now follows the ticked language at once.
6. **What-if:** the amount box takes the cursor when opened.
7. **Mutation checks** for the figures of Runs 97-100 (section 5).

## 11. Run 101 (same session): one shared list of payments still due

**What changed.** `openCommitments` (App.jsx) is now the one place that lists scheduled payments with no record yet. Safe to Spend, the month-end forecast and the "until your next income" figure use it. The run-out day follows through Safe to Spend and the forecast.

**Why.** A confirmed double count. If someone typed a bill payment in ahead as an ordinary entry (same category, same name, same amount), Safe to Spend counted it once (rule F1, since Run 22), but the forecast and the until-income figure counted it twice: once as the entry and once as the bill.

**Visible effect.** Only for people who type bill payments in ahead. For them the forecast goes down and the until-income figure goes up, by that payment. Before, those figures were wrong by exactly that amount. Safe to Spend does not change. The forecast's range check uses the same rule, so whether the range is shown can change. This extends an existing rule to two more figures; no new accounting rule.

**Hand example (in the test).** 16 Nov, 400 in accounts, electricity 60 due on the 20th, salary on the 25th (9 days). Typed in ahead for the 20th: 400 - 60 = 340, 37.77 a day. Old code: 400 - 60 - 60 = 280.

## 13. Run 102 (17 Nov, autonomous block): "Until your next income" counts entries dated ahead as money

- What was wrong (CONFIRMED, my Run 97 design, found in the review in section 12): the figure starts from what the accounts hold, which is all money. The entries dated ahead that it takes off came from the spending view. That view keeps only the interest of a loan payment, leaves out money lent to a person, and keeps only the part of a cost nobody will pay back. So an entry like that, dated before the income, made the figure too high by the missing part.
- Hand example: card 200, an expense of 30 tomorrow and 50 lent to a friend tomorrow, income in 3 days. Before: 200 - 30 = 170, 56.66 a day. Now: 200 - 30 - 50 = 120, 40.00 a day.
- Fix: App.jsx `calcUntilPay` takes the raw entries (`opts.cashTx`) for the sum of entries dated ahead; Home passes them. The next income date is found as before, so a refund dated ahead is never taken as "the next income".
- Effect: the figure can only go down, and only when such an entry is dated ahead. Safe to Spend, the forecast and balances do not change.
