## 2. What changed, and why

1. **One line under Safe to Spend: "Until your next income on {date}: {amount} in your accounts after the payments due ({per day} a day)."**
   Why: Safe to Spend follows the calendar month. The day after pay day it can say 102 a day for 5 days while the salary has to last 29 days at 47 a day. The line shows the second figure beside the first. Safe to Spend itself is not changed.
   Shown only when accounts exist and the next income date is known. Silent otherwise.
2. **"Paid from" (the account chips) moved from "More details" into the main Add form.**
   Why: an entry with no account moves no balance. The chooser was folded away, and the very first entry has no "same as last time" to fall back on, so balances drifted from the start. This is the root of the Run 98 and 99 repairs.
   Cost: one more row in the form for people who use accounts. People without accounts see no change.
3. **Safe to Spend card names loan payments still due this month.**
   Finding (confirmed by reading the code and by a test): Safe to Spend takes out recurring payments, but never loan payments from the Debts page. With a 300 loan payment due on the 28th, the figure is 300 too high until that day.
   What I did: the card now says "Loan payments still due this month: X. They are not taken out of this figure, unless you also set them up as recurring payments."
   What I did not do: subtract them. That changes an established rule, and people who have the loan in both places would be counted twice. Owner decision D9.
4. **Fix of my own Run 99 defect:** in Azerbaijani and Russian "What if I buy something?" sat glued to "How is this calculated?" on one row. Found by screenshot. Own row now; regression check added.
5. **First launch:** the line under the app name kept changing language after a language was ticked. It now follows the ticked language at once.
6. **What-if:** the amount box takes the cursor when opened.
7. **Mutation checks** for the figures of Runs 97-100 (section 5).
