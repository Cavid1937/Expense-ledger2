## 6. New capability

"What if I buy something?" - type an amount; the card answers what would be left and the new per-day figure, or by how much it is too much. Nothing is saved. It reuses the two existing figures, so it adds no new accounting rule.

Smaller option considered and rejected: no feature at all (people can subtract in their head). Kept because the per-day change is the part people cannot do in their head, and the cost is one small function.

## 15. Privacy: where data can go (read from the code)

| Flow | When | What leaves the phone | To |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entries, budgets, goals | Always | Nothing | - |
| Exchange rate | Entering another currency | Currency code and date | api.frankfurter.dev |
| Update check, page load | Opening with a connection | Ordinary web request (IP, time) | Vercel |
| User count | Daily, ON unless switched off | Date, platform, language, version, random number that changes monthly | Supabase (EU) |
| "Help improve the app" | Only if switched on | Place names used twice + category, screen-open counts, bank-notice layouts with numbers removed, crash text | Supabase (EU) |
| Account | Only if signed in with Google | Full copy of the data, name, e-mail | Supabase (EU) |
| Drive backup | Only if switched on | Backup file | The person's own Google Drive (hidden app folder) |
| Bank-notice reader | Only if allowed (Android) | Nothing; read on the phone | - |
| Statement import, receipt photos | When used | Nothing; photos stay in the phone's storage and are in no backup | - |
| Report a problem | When sent | What the person typed | Supabase (EU) |

The in-app privacy text and `public/privacy.html` describe these flows. One sentence did not (lock screen); fixed.
Server access rules, read on 17 November 2026 (read-only query of the policies): row-level security is on for all 8 tables. `ledgers` (account copies): read, add, change and delete only where the row's owner is the signed-in person. The 7 reporting tables: anonymous add only, no read. VERIFIED by reading the rules; not attacked in a live test. Supabase's own security check lists two warnings: the account-delete function runs with elevated rights (intended), and leaked-password protection is off (matters only once e-mail sign-up exists).
Not verified: that the built app makes no other request (checked by reading source addresses only). Low risk noted: the anonymous reporting tables have no rate limit.
Claims the app must not make: "local-only", "nothing ever leaves your phone", "no servers".

## 17. Owner decisions

D1. Safe to Spend on the pay cycle. Hand example, salary 1,200 on the 25th, today 16 November:
- Today: account 400, bill 60 due on the 20th. Until-income figure: 400 - 60 = 340 over 9 days = 37.77 a day. Safe to Spend shows nothing (no November income yet).
- 25 November, salary recorded; November spending so far 520; saving 200. Safe to Spend (calendar month): 1,200 - 200 - 520 = 480 over 6 days = 80.00 a day. But this salary must last until 25 December.
- Pay-cycle figure on 25 November: account 340 + 1,200 = 1,540 - 200 saving - 210 bills before 25 December = 1,130 over 30 days = 37.66 a day.
- The calendar figure says 80 a day; the money supports about 38. My recommendation: make the pay-cycle figure the main one when accounts and a next income date exist; keep the monthly figure as "this month's budget" for everyone else. I will not change it without your yes.

D2. Positioning: choose A, B, C or D (section 9). Recommended A.

D3. Publish Runs 97 + 98 + 99 to the live site. Recommended yes, after the full suite and offline check pass. Rollback is `2626caf`.

D4. APK 99 and a week on your phone (notice reader, widgets, new cards). Recommended yes after D3.

D5. Google Play account (25 USD once; real package name instead of `com.example...`). Needed before any outside tester on Android.

D6. E-mail sign-up (needs a mail sender). Recommended: wait; Google sign-in covers Android.
