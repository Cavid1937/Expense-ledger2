## What changed
1. **Category budget box** (screens/goals.jsx): Save folds the box closed (0.2 s) and then saves, so it no longer vanishes in one frame.
   I could not reproduce an "instant close before typing" on desktop Chrome; a phone check is still needed.
2. **Date ranges inside the calendar** (screens/pickers.jsx `PickRange`, ledger.jsx): one sheet with All time, 7/30/90 days and a two-tap
   custom range. Tapping the chosen range again, or All time, removes the filter: that is the way back.
3. **Bank notices** (App.jsx, new screens/banknotice.jsx): readable payments are first saved to a waiting list
   (`expense_ledger_v5__bankq`), then a bell notice and a small "Review" bar appear. Nothing opens by itself. The review sheet is short:
   each payment with Spent/Received and a category, one account (remembered), then Add / Not mine / Later, with Undo both ways.
4. **Import / Export** (screens/settings.jsx, i18n.js): look and grouping only. "Back up and move", a "Backup file" card with the
   where-it-goes text behind an (i) tip; Google Drive consent text stays visible on purpose. Page 1946 → 1758 px tall.
5. **Reminder time** (screens/pickers.jsx, styles.js): two scroll wheels (hours 00–23, minutes 00–59). They snap to a row, curve like a
   drum, the big time on top follows, each new row gives one short phone tick (none on opening). Save stores it; X / Escape / back keep the
   old time. Screen readers hear two spin buttons (Hour, Minute); arrow keys, Page Up/Down, Home/End work. Reduced motion drops the curve.
6. **Screen audit** (docs/RUN105_SCREEN_AUDIT.md): every tab and Settings page in several states, en/az/ru automatic checks + my own look.
   Fixed: a **misleading figure** on Analytics (last month's *spending* sat under "Monthly surplus" and read as last month's surplus; now
   under Spent as "spent the same days last month"); one-entry days no longer repeat their amount as a day total; the budget "Last months"
   toggle looks like a control; the info-sheet X got the larger touch area. Five more findings are written down as proposals (not built).
7. **Claude-feel checklist** (docs/CLAUDE_FEEL_CHECKLIST.md): one page I must tick for every new feature (motion, touch, layers, words,
   proof), so the feel is not forgotten again.
8. APP_RELEASE "105 (2026-11-17)".
9. **Confirmed defect found by the full suite, fixed** (commit 9700e3c): after the Import/Export page got shorter (item 4), opening
   "Encrypted backup" left the second passphrase box under the bottom tab bar; a tap there hit the tab bar, so the two passphrases
   never matched and the backup could not be made. The form now glides to the middle of the screen and puts the cursor in the first box.
   Found by bisect (799550a pass, a24b4ba pass, 001aa06 fail). New check R105-E: a tap on either box reaches the box.
