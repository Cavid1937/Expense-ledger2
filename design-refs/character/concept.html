Qəpik

A small round coin with a face, named after the smallest coin in Azerbaijan. Same in all three languages. Colours are the app's Ivory theme, so it belongs to the app rather than sitting on top of it.

CalmMost of the time. Says nothing.
GladA goal reached, a month under budget.
CuriousEmpty screens: "Nothing here yet."
CarefulClose to a limit. Never sad, never blaming.

Where it would appear

First launch and empty screens
Greets you when choosing a language; fills empty Goals, Debts, Entries lists.
Real milestones only
Goal reached, debt paid off, a full month recorded. Once, then gone.

Rules, so it never hurts trust

  • Never next to a balance, amount or chart. Numbers stay plain and serious.
  • Never jokes about debt, never guilt, never "you spent too much!" faces.
  • Silent: no speech bubbles with advice. Its face does the talking; the text stays the app's normal plain text.
  • One switch in Settings to turn it off. Respects "reduce motion".
  • Same shape at 24 px and 200 px, so it also works as the widget and notification picture later.
